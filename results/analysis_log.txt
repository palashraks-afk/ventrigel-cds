[2/9]  Does the subgroup effect exist? (the test nobody ran)
==============================================================================
  The trial compared each stratum against its own baseline and observed
  that one reached significance while the other did not. That is not a
  test of effect modification. Below is the comparison of the strata
  against each other, which neither the trial nor earlier versions of
  this analysis performed.

  endpoint          early     late  difference               95% CI        p
  lvesv              9.30    -7.60      -16.90        [-32.2, -1.6]   0.0342 *
  ef                -3.80    -0.60        3.20          [-1.1, 7.5]   0.1329
  viable_mass      -10.50    15.00       25.50        [-15.1, 66.1]   0.1695
  mlwhfq             0.70   -15.10      -15.80        [-49.9, 18.3]   0.3320
  scar               2.30    -1.00       -3.30         [-12.9, 6.3]   0.4374
  bnp               -6.70   -18.30      -11.60        [-51.7, 28.5]   0.5404
  lvedv             -1.20    -7.60       -6.40        [-32.3, 19.5]   0.5913
  six_min_walk      40.50    50.90       10.40        [-52.4, 73.2]   0.7231
  nyha              -0.30    -0.30        0.00          [-0.9, 0.9]   1.0000

  Multiplicity across 9 interaction tests:
    endpoint              p  Bonferroni p  BH threshold   passes
    lvesv            0.0342         0.308        0.0056       no
    ef               0.1329         1.000        0.0111       no
    viable_mass      0.1695         1.000        0.0167       no
    mlwhfq           0.3320         1.000        0.0222       no
    ... 5 further endpoints, all p > 0.3

  Ejection fraction is determined by the two volumes, and viable mass and scar fraction partition the same myocardium on the same scan, so the nine endpoints are not nine independent questions. Collapsing each linked family to one test gives 5. Even at that denominator the Bonferroni threshold is 0.0100, which the end-systolic volume interaction at p = 0.034 still fails.

  Baseline balance: 8/8 measures balanced (all p >= 0.46).
    The strata were not randomized against each other, so this matters.
      six_min_walk   early   442.4  late   418.0  p=0.633
      lvesv          early   156.8  late   142.3  p=0.682
      lvedv          early   245.3  late   217.8  p=0.478

  Regression to the mean: The early stratum began with the higher lv end-systolic volume (156.8 vs 142.3 mL) and changed by +9.3 mL. Regression to the mean would have moved it down, so the observed pattern runs opposite to that artifact.

  VERDICT: One endpoint of 9 shows a nominally significant interaction (lvesv, p = 0.034). It does not survive Bonferroni or Benjamini-Hochberg correction. The strata are balanced at baseline, and the pattern is not explained by regression to the mean. The finding is therefore suggestive and fragile: strong enough to justify designing a trial around, not strong enough to assert as established.

[3/9]  What does an untreated patient do? (external control anchors)
==============================================================================
  The Phase I was single-arm, so its comparator is missing. Rather than
  sweeping an arbitrary range, control-arm change is anchored to published
  control and placebo arms. Indexed volumes converted at BSA = 1.9 m2.

  trial             year    phase  measure      published  absolute mL     n
  TIME              2012    acute   LVESVI     +4.3 mL/m2         +8.2    37
  PRESERVATION-I    2016    acute   LVEDVI    +11.7 mL/m2        +22.2   102
  EMPRESS-MI        2025    acute   LVESVI     -7.8 mL/m2        -14.8    52
  FOCUS-CCTRN       2012  chronic   LVESVI     +0.0 mL/m2         +0.0    28
  FOCUS-HF          2011  chronic    LVESV        -9.9 mL         -9.9    10

  BSA check: VentriGel's 148.5 mL baseline implies 78.2 mL/m2, against 65.0 mL/m2 in the chronic
  FOCUS-CCTRN population. Higher, as expected for a dilated post-MI cohort,
  and not so far off as to invalidate the conversion.

  EARLY stratum comparator: central +8.2 mL, range [-14.8, +8.2]
    Acute post-MI natural history is era-dependent. Older trials show dilation (TIME +4.3 mL/m^2), the most recent shows reverse remodeling on contemporary therapy (EMPRESS-MI -7.8 mL/m^2). The range spans both signs deliberately.

  LATE stratum comparator: central +0.0 mL, range [-9.9, +9.9]
    Chronic ischemic populations are stable over six months. FOCUS-CCTRN, the closest match on chronicity, ejection fraction criterion and delivery route, observed no change. FOCUS-HF (n = 10) sets the pessimistic bound.

  The acute anchors disagree in SIGN, which is the finding rather than a
  defect. Older cohorts dilated; the 2022-2024 cohort on contemporary
  therapy underwent reverse remodeling. Post-MI natural history is
  era-dependent, so no single control number is defensible for the early
  stratum and the range is carried through every downstream result.

[6/9]  Power is not probability of success
==============================================================================
  A trial sized for 80% power at a point estimate is not an 80% trial when
  the point estimate rests on eight patients. Assurance integrates power
  over the uncertainty in the effect, which is what a sponsor deciding
  whether to fund the trial actually needs.

   N total  nominal power  assurance
        52         60.5%     53.2%
        92         84.8%     67.7%
       108         90.0%     71.2%
       150         96.9%     77.6%
       200         99.3%     82.1%
       300        100.0%     86.9%
       500        100.0%     90.8%
      1000        100.0%     93.7%

  Ceiling: 97.5%. No sample size does better, because that is the
  share of plausible effects that point toward benefit at all.

  Enrollment required for a target probability of success:
    50% assurance: 48 patients
    60% assurance: 68 patients
    70% assurance: 104 patients
    80% assurance: 174 patients
    85% assurance: 252 patients
    90% assurance: 442 patients

  This is the single most consequential correction in the analysis. The
  design that looked like 92 patients is a coin flip plus a little; genuine
  80% probability of success needs roughly 174.

[8/9]  Which assumption the conclusion actually rests on
==============================================================================
  Scenario analysis over the literature-anchored control range:

  scenario                                c_early   c_late    N unsel    N enr      adv
  no control drift (naive)                    0.0      0.0 no benefit       92 unbounded
  anchored central                            8.2      0.0      1,046       92    11.4x
  modern early care (EMPRESS-MI)            -14.8      0.0 no benefit       92 unbounded
  pessimistic late (FOCUS-HF)                 8.2     -9.9 no benefit no benefit        -
  worst case for enrichment                 -14.8     -9.9 no benefit no benefit        -

  The enriched design is unaffected by the early-stratum assumption, since
  it enrolls no early patients. It is entirely dependent on the late one.
  FOCUS-CCTRN (n=28, chronic, LVEF<=45%, same delivery route) says zero and
  the design stands. FOCUS-HF (n=10) says -9.9 mL, under which VentriGel's
  -7.6 mL is smaller than natural history and there is no effect at all.
  That one number decides the project.

  Winner's-curse discount swept across its full range:
   discount    effect   N enriched   N unselected
       0.40      3.04          312          3,668
       0.50      3.80          202          2,348
       0.60      4.56          140          1,632
       0.70      5.32          104          1,200
       0.80      6.08           80            920
       0.90      6.84           64            726
       1.00      7.60           52            590

  No single discount is defended. Across the whole range the enriched
  trial stays between roughly 50 and 300 patients, which is the claim.

[9/9]  Cost and recruitment feasibility
==============================================================================
  Unit costs (planning assumptions, not measurements): fixed $2,000,000; $65,000/patient; $2,500/screen; $12,000/site-month; 6 sites at 0.75/site/month

  lvesv:
    unselected: N=1,046, $87.4M
    cheapest viable: e=1.00, N=92, screened=172, 47 months, $11.6M
  six_min_walk:
    unselected: N=86, $9.6M
    cheapest viable: e=0.98, N=56, screened=103, 32 months, $8.1M

  The recommended design (80% assurance, N=174): 326 screened, 82 months, $19.6M at 6 sites.
  Sites required to finish on a given calendar:
    24 months total: 29 sites
    30 months total: 21 sites
    36 months total: 17 sites
    48 months total: 12 sites

  The six sites that ran the Phase I cannot deliver this trial. That is
  a recruitment finding, not a statistical one, and it is the constraint
  a sponsor would hit first.
