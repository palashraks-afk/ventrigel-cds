[2/10]  Does the subgroup effect exist? (the test nobody ran)
==============================================================================
  The trial compared each stratum against its own baseline and observed
  that one reached significance while the other did not. That is not a
  test of effect modification.

  endpoint          early     late      diff              95% CI        p
  lvesv              9.30    -7.60    -16.90       [-32.2, -1.6]   0.0342 *
  ef                -3.80    -0.60      3.20         [-1.1, 7.5]   0.1329
  viable_mass      -10.50    15.00     25.50       [-15.1, 66.1]   0.1695
  mlwhfq             0.70   -15.10    -15.80       [-49.9, 18.3]   0.3320
  scar               2.30    -1.00     -3.30        [-12.9, 6.3]   0.4374
  bnp               -6.70   -18.30    -11.60       [-51.7, 28.5]   0.5404
  lvedv             -1.20    -7.60     -6.40       [-32.3, 19.5]   0.5913
  six_min_walk      40.50    50.90     10.40       [-52.4, 73.2]   0.7231
  nyha              -0.30    -0.30      0.00         [-0.9, 0.9]   1.0000

  Multiplicity across 9 interaction tests:
    lvesv          p=0.0342  Bonferroni p=0.308  BH threshold=0.0056  passes=no
    ef             p=0.1329  Bonferroni p=1.000  BH threshold=0.0111  passes=no
    viable_mass    p=0.1695  Bonferroni p=1.000  BH threshold=0.0167  passes=no
    ... 6 further endpoints, all p > 0.16

  Ejection fraction is determined by the two volumes, and viable mass and scar fraction partition the same myocardium on the same scan, so the nine endpoints are not nine independent questions. Collapsing each linked family to one test gives 5. Even at that denominator the Bonferroni threshold is 0.0100, which the end-systolic volume interaction at p = 0.034 still fails.

  Two further caveats on the test itself, neither of which can be resolved
  without patient-level data:
    - It is a comparison of change scores, not an ANCOVA adjusting for
      baseline. With balanced baselines ANCOVA is the standard and more
      powerful choice, so p = 0.034 is probably conservative.
    - The family is nine endpoints at the 6-month visit. The trial also
      reported 1- and 3-month visits; counting those would enlarge the
      family and weaken the result further. Six months is used because it
      is the prespecified secondary-endpoint timepoint.

  Baseline balance: 8/8 measures balanced (minimum p = 0.46).
  Regression to the mean: The early stratum began with the higher lv end-systolic volume (156.8 vs 142.3 mL) and changed by +9.3 mL. Regression to the mean would have moved it down, so the observed pattern runs opposite to that artifact.

  VERDICT: One endpoint of 9 shows a nominally significant interaction (lvesv, p = 0.034). It does not survive Bonferroni or Benjamini-Hochberg correction. The strata are balanced at baseline, and the pattern is not explained by regression to the mean. The finding is therefore suggestive and fragile: strong enough to justify designing a trial around, not strong enough to assert as established.

[8/10]  Confirming the claim itself, not just the effect
==============================================================================
  The enriched design enrolls only late patients. It can show the therapy
  works in that stratum; it can never show that timing matters, because it
  contains no early patients. But 'treat late, not early' is the actual
  claim. Confirming it needs a 2x2 trial powered on the interaction.

  scenario                contrast  N/cell   N total  enriched ref   ratio
  no control drift           12.68      31       124            92    1.3x
  anchored controls           6.55     110       440            92    4.8x

  Anchoring halves the contrast, because most of the early stratum's apparent
  harm turns out to be natural history rather than a failure of treatment.
  The interaction design then costs about 440 patients -- close to the 406 an
  80%-assurance enriched trial needs. For roughly the same money a sponsor can
  answer the question they actually have instead of half of it.

[10/10]  The number a sponsor actually needs
==============================================================================
  Everything above is conditional on the interaction being real. It rests on
  one nominally significant result out of nine that survives no correction.
  Unconditional probability of success is that prior times the assurance.

        N     30%     40%     50%     60%     80%    100%
       92   18.9%   25.2%   31.5%   37.8%   50.4%   63.0%
      174   21.6%   28.9%   36.1%   43.3%   57.7%   72.1%
      300   23.3%   31.1%   38.8%   46.6%   62.1%   77.7%
      406   24.0%   32.0%   40.0%   48.0%   64.0%   80.0%
      800   25.2%   33.5%   41.9%   50.3%   67.1%   83.9%

  The prior enters multiplicatively, so no sample size lifts the programme
  above it. At an even-odds prior the best achievable is about 42%, and the
  marginal return on patients beyond roughly 400 is close to nothing. That is
  the argument for spending the next increment of money on measuring the
  control-arm comparator rather than on more patients.

  Recommended design: N=406, 761 screened, $42.1M.
  Sites required: 68 for 24 months, 49 for 30 months, 38 for 36 months, 27 for 48 months
